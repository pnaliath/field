Field
a room seen from the mix position — across is pan, up is frequency, into the room is depth

Every sound is a shape. Where they overlap, they mask.

A track that moves around the stereo field is drawn as several shapes, one per position, each lighting up only when it plays. Widening the track pulls them apart.
